## B. City facts

### B1. UNESCO

- Inscribed **2006** as **Harar Jugol, the Fortified Historic Town**
- Core zone **48 hectares** (entire walled city)

### B2. Why Harar matters

- **Faith:** Often described as a major holy city of Islam; **82 mosques**, **102 shrines**; three mosques dating to the 10th century; centre for propagation of Islam in the Horn of Africa. Soften “fourth holy city” unless Commission wants that phrase.
- **Trade:** Late 16th–19th c. trade centre between coast and Ethiopian interior highlands.
- **Living culture:** Functioning community — *afocha* organization, *gey gar* housing, weaving, basket making, book binding; Harari language and practices maintained.

### B3. City of Peace

- UNESCO **Cities for Peace Prize** cited as **2002** (verify year).
- Multiethnic / multiconfessional coexistence (Oromo, Amhara, Argobba, Harari, Somali, etc.).
- Tied to *afocha* and history as Islamic urban centre in a wider Christian empire — not a hollow tourism slogan.
- Keep Draft 01 nuance: not “never conflict.”

### B4. Scale visitors notice

| Element | Figure |
|---|---|
| Wall length | ~**3,500 m** (confirm) |
| Gates | **5 historic** + later **Harar Gate** = six openings total |
| Mosques | **82** (UNESCO OUV) |
| Elevation | ~**1,885 m** (confirm) |

### B5. Seasons / climate

- Dry season **Oct–Mar** best for walking + evening hyena visits.
- Subtropical highland (Köppen Cwb); often **20–27°C**; pleasant year-round with warm days / cooler evenings.
- Oct–Apr dry sunny highland conditions noted for exploring.

### B6. Safety & respect

- Conservative dress at religious sites.
- Remove shoes entering mosques; women cover heads at Jamia / active prayer spaces.
- Ask before photographing people; respect prayer times.
- Museum note: often no touching manuscripts; shoes off inside some museums — confirm per venue.

### B7. Arrival

| Mode | Detail |
|---|---|
| Air + road | Dire Dawa → Harar ~**50–51 km**, ~**49–60 min** by car/taxi |
| Train | Addis → Dire Dawa newer track ~**450 km**, ~**7.5 hours**; then ~1 hour road to Harar |
| Bajaj fares / hours | **UNKNOWN** |

### B8. Getting around

- Jugol on foot (narrow alleys).
- Bajaj outside walls / newer Harar / Dire Dawa links.
- No expectation of cars inside historic alleys.

## D. Experiences (6)

### D1. Three-Round Harari Coffee Ceremony

- **Category:** `Experience · Cultural & Spiritual`
- **Summary:** Home/café ritual — roast, grind, *jebena*; rounds *abol*, *tona*, *baraka*
- **Duration:** 45–90 min; late morning or late afternoon
- **Etiquette:** Right hand; don’t leave before *baraka* without polite excuse; lean into incense smoke
- **Contact:** Guesthouse / guide; public fixed schedule UNKNOWN

### D2. Hyena Feeding at Dusk (Northern Site)

- **Category:** `Experience · Wildlife & Adventure`
- **Summary:** Nightly feeding; hyena doors in walls; wild animals, not a zoo
- **Duration:** 30–60 min from ~18:30
- **Named host in draft:** Abbas Yusuf — **confirm before publishing**
- **Contact:** Hotel / visitor desk

### D3. Harari Basket Weaving with Women’s Guilds

- **Category:** `Experience · Artisans & Crafts`
- **Summary:** *Ge mot* coil basketry; marriage/domestic aesthetics; three women’s associations in old city (per draft)
- **Duration:** 1–2 hours afternoon
- **Contact:** Guide → cooperative; specific booking contacts UNKNOWN

### D4. Shoa Gate Market Food & Spice Walk

- **Category:** `Experience · Food & Markets`
- **Summary:** Christian Market / Shoa Gate — *ful*, *etan*, spices, medicine ingredients
- **Duration:** 1–2 hours; ~16:00–18:00 peak
- **Contact:** Visitor desk / guide

### D5. Night Walk Through Lantern-Lit Alleys of Jugol

- **Category:** `Experience · Cultural & Spiritual`
- **Summary:** Quiet evening walk; murals, call to prayer, living neighbourhood
- **Duration:** 45–90 min; ~19:00–21:00
- **Safety:** Prefer guide/group; avoid unlit alone
- **Greeting note:** “salaam” / “aman be diusk” — verify Harari phrasing with Commission

### D6. Traditional Harari House Visit (Gey Gar)

- **Category:** `Experience · Cultural & Spiritual`
- **Summary:** Lived-in or museum house — raised platforms, storage, basket rules
- **Duration:** 30–60 min daytime
- **Contact:** Guide for private home; or Rimbaud / Sherif as formal architecture visits

## B. City facts

### B1. UNESCO

- **Name:** Harar Jugol, the Fortified Historic Town  
- **Inscribed:** 2006  
- **Criteria:** (ii), (iii), (iv), (v)  
- **Core zone:** 48 ha (entire walled historic town)  
- **Source:** [UNESCO WHC 1189](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1189/)

### B2. Why Harar matters

- **Faith:** Fourth holiest city of Islam in local/regional tradition (after Mecca, Medina, Jerusalem); dense mosques/shrines; continuous scholarship and pilgrimage  
- **Trade:** Late 16th–19th c. hub linking Red Sea / Indian Ocean coast ↔ Ethiopian highlands (coffee, spices, textiles; African, Arab, Indian networks)  
- **Living culture:** Functioning historic community — houses, weaving, basketry, book-binding, language, social organisation inside the walls  

### B3. City of Peace / hospitality

- Arabic: **Madīnat al-Awliyā’** (“City of Saints”) — density of saintly shrines + early Muslim scholars  
- “City of Peace” = documented intercommunal coexistence + neighbourly mediation/hospitality — not a marketing slogan  
- UNESCO City of Peace recognition in the **early 2000s** — exact year still verify  

### B4. Scale visitors notice

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Walls | 13th–16th c.; enclose ~48 ha; circumference ~**3.5–4.5 km** (secondary); height commonly **3–5 m** |
| Gates | **Five historic**; later modern entry = Harar Gate / Duke’s Gate *(do not merge with Shoa/Asmadin)* |
| Mosques/shrines | UNESCO **82** mosques (3 from 10th c.), **102** shrines; local symbolic figures (e.g. 99) secondary |
| Elevation | ~**1,885 m** plateau |

### B5. Seasons / climate

- Milder than surrounding lowlands  
- **Best walking:** roughly **Oct–Mar** (esp. Nov–Feb); daytime mid-20s °C, clearer skies  
- **Wetter:** Apr–May and parts of Jul–Sep; afternoon showers; uneven lanes less pleasant  
- Cool evenings year-round  

### B6. Safety & respect

- Conservative dress in Jugol; shoulders/knees; scarf useful for women near faith sites  
- Ask before photographing people; no prayer photography; mosque/shrine interiors often restricted  
- Assume non-Muslim interior access restricted unless guide confirms; shoes off at thresholds  
- Avoid Friday midday prayer congestion  
- Guide strongly recommended for first visits and after dark (unlit, maze-like)  

### B7. Arrival

| Mode | Detail |
|---|---|
| Air + road | Addis → Dire Dawa (DIR) ~1 h flight; then ~**50–55 km** / **1–1.5 h** road to Harar |
| Train + road | Overnight Addis → Dire Dawa (schedules change), then same road transfer |
| Road only | Buses/minibuses; commonly **9–12 h** |
| Note | Harar has **no airport** |

### B8. Getting around

- Jugol: **foot only**; no motorised traffic in historic core  
- Outside: bajaj standard; minibuses/taxis for longer local trips (e.g. hyena sites)

### C1. Five Historic Gates of Jugol

- **Local names:** Suqutat/Sanga; Argob/Erer; Assumiy/Fallana; Asmadin/Shewa; Badri/Buda  
- **Category:** Historic Gates  
- **Summary:** Five original 16th-c. gates; neighbourhoods + trade routes  
- **Notes:** Freely accessible; no fee; daylight wall circuit; Erer & Fallana link to hyena sites  
- **How:** Start Harar Gate → circuit with guide → Erer/Fallana if continuing after dark  

### C2. Jamia Mosque

- **Local names:** Jami Mosque; Grand Mosque of Harar; *(Grok also: Amir Nur Mesjid — verify)*  
- **Category:** Mosques & Shrines  
- **Summary:** Principal Friday mosque; largest inside walls  
- **Tradition:** Site from 12th/early 13th c.; remodelled 16th c.; twin minarets  
- **Location:** Central Jugol, near Amir Uga Street  
- **Visitor:** Exterior only for non-Muslims in most cases; avoid Friday midday  

### C3. Arthur Rimbaud Cultural Center

- **Local names:** Maison Rimbaud; Rimbaud’s House  
- **Category:** Museums & Palaces  
- **Summary:** Restored Indian-merchant house; museum of Rimbaud’s trading years (1880–1891)  
- **Accuracy:** House he occupied **destroyed 1915** — current building is associated museum, not proven residence  
- **Location:** Near centre, close to Feres Magala  
- **Notes:** Modest fee reported in older sources — confirm on site; women’s association / basket area nearby  

### C4. Gidir Magala (Main Market)

- **Local names:** Magaalaa Guddoo; Muslim Market (historic)  
- **Category:** Markets  
- **Summary:** Historic central market; main butchery; spices and everyday trade  
- **Detail:** Black kites swoop for meat scraps — distinctive visitor observation  
- **Nearby:** Mekina Girgir (tailors)  

### C5. Feres Magala (Horse Square)

- **Local names:** Horse Market / Horse Square  
- **Category:** Markets / Viewpoints  
- **Summary:** Main public square; historic horse market; orientation landmark  
- **Notes:** Monument depicting five gates; near Medhane Alem Cathedral; start/end for walks  

### C6. Sherif Harar City Museum

- **Local names:** Ras Tafari’s House; Abdallah Sherif Museum  
- **Category:** Museums & Palaces  
- **Summary:** Former Ras Tafari residence; private collection — manuscripts, weapons, jewellery, basketry, book-restoration workshop  
- **Location:** Central Jugol, near Rimbaud Center  
- **Visit:** ~30–60 min; modest contribution expected  

### C7. Traditional Harari Houses (Gey Gar / Gidir Gar)

- **Local names:** Gey Gar; Gidir Gar  
- **Category:** Living Heritage / Domestic Architecture  
- **Summary:** Courtyard houses with *nadaba* platforms and basket-lined walls — UNESCO’s “most spectacular” heritage element  
- **Visitor:** Invitation/arranged only; shoes off; tip or basket purchase customary  

### C8. Hyena Feeding Sites

- **Local names:** Hyena Man sites  
- **Category:** Living Tradition / Wildlife Cultural Practice  
- **Summary:** Nightly feeding of wild spotted hyenas outside the walls  
- **Sites:** North of Fallana Gate; east of Erer Gate  
- **Timing:** After dusk, commonly from ~**18:30**  
- **Notes:** Small payment expected; animals remain wild; not for young children / uneasy visitors  

### C9. Tomb of Said Ali Hamdogn

- **Category:** Mosques & Shrines  
- **Summary:** Small whitewashed shrine of a 12th-c. religious leader; tradition of a siege well  
- **Visitor:** Generally more approachable; shoes if entering; no formal fee  

### C10. Tomb of Sheikh Abadir (Aw Abadir)

- **Local names:** Aw Abadir; Maqam of Shaykh Abadir  
- **Category:** Mosques & Shrines  
- **Summary:** Founding saint / second emir; major pilgrimage compound  
- **Tradition:** Umar al-Rida; early 13th c. from Hijaz  
- **Visitor:** Non-Muslims usually exterior only; Thursday-night gatherings sometimes limited access — confirm locally

## D. Experiences (7)

### D1. Three-round Harari Coffee Ceremony

- **Category:** Experience · Cultural & Spiritual | Food & Markets  
- **Summary:** Home/courtyard ceremony — roast, grind, three rounds, incense, conversation  
- **Duration:** 45–90 min; morning or late afternoon  
- **Etiquette note (Grok):** Declining after first round “understood” — **conflicts** with packs that stress staying for all three; confirm locally  
- **Contact:** Visitor desk / local host  

### D2. Evening Hyena Feeding outside the Walls

- **Category:** Experience · Wildlife & Adventure | Cultural & Spiritual  
- **Sites:** Fallana (north) + Erer (east)  
- **Duration:** 30–60 min; from ~18:30  
- **Framing:** Living arrangement, not staged show; observation preferred; participate only if invited  
- **Safety:** No bare-finger meat; no alcohol; follow handler exactly  

### D3. Basket-Weaving Visit with Harari Women

- **Category:** Experience · Artisans & Crafts  
- **Duration:** 20–45 min daylight  
- **Context:** Women’s cooperative near Rimbaud Center or household courtyards  
- **Etiquette:** Buy directly; ask before photos  

### D4. Morning Market Walk through Gidir Magala and Mekina Girgir

- **Category:** Experience · Food & Markets  
- **Duration:** 1–1.5 h; before mid-morning  
- **Route:** Butchery/spice lanes → tailor street; watch for kites  
- **Contact:** Visitor desk / local host  

### D5. Night Cultural Walk inside Jugol

- **Category:** Experience · Cultural & Spiritual  
- **Duration:** 45–75 min after evening prayer  
- **Notes:** Guided only; torch sparingly; pairs with hyena or late coffee  

### D6. Traditional House Interior Visit

- **Category:** Experience · Cultural & Spiritual | Artisans & Crafts  
- **Duration:** 30–60 min  
- **Themes:** *Nadaba* hierarchy; niches; baskets/enamel; coffee often follows  

### D7. Spice and Coffee Market Exploration outside the Main Gate

- **Category:** Experience · Food & Markets  
- **Duration:** 30–60 min mornings  
- **Location:** Immediately outside Harar Gate — beans, berbere, incense  
- **Note:** Sensory counterpoint to denser Gidir Magala inside

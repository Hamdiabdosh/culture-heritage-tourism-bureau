## B. City facts

### B1. UNESCO status

- Inscribed as a UNESCO World Heritage Site in **2006**.

### B2. Why Harar matters

- **Faith:** Regarded as one of the most important Islamic cities in the world; **82 mosques** (some dating to the 10th century) and **over 100 shrines**.
- **Trade:** Vital commercial crossroads connecting the Horn of Africa with the Arabian Peninsula and international trade routes.
- **Living culture:** Home to the Harari people — distinct language, traditions, basketry, and the custom of nightly hyena feeding.

### B3. City of Peace / hospitality

- Often called a **“City of Peace and Tolerance”**, reflecting centuries of coexistence among diverse religious and ethnic communities, rooted in mutual respect and community organization.
- *(Editorial note from Draft 01 still applies: do not erase conflict history or promise a frictionless visit.)*

### B4. Rough scale visitors notice

| Element | Gemini draft |
|---|---|
| Walls | Surrounded by a ~**4 m** defensive stone wall (Jugol) — height cite/confirm |
| Gates | **Five** historical gates (historically corresponding to five quarters) |
| Mosques / shrines | Approx. **82 mosques** and **over 100 shrines** (UNESCO/heritage-based) |

### B5. Best seasons / climate

- **Best:** Dry season **October–March** for comfortable walking.
- **Climate:** Mild semi-arid / highland vs lower elevations; warm days, cooler evenings.

### B6. Safety & respect

- Conservative dress; cover shoulders and knees.
- Always ask before photographing residents, especially elders and women.
- Mosques/shrines may have entry protocols; non-Muslims should check access with local guides.

### B7. How to arrive

| Mode | Detail |
|---|---|
| Air + road | Fly **Dire Dawa (Aba Tenna Dejazmach Yilma International Airport)** → minibus/taxi/private car to Harar; ~**1 hour** for ~**50 km** |
| Road from Addis | Buses / private; typically **8–10+ hours** depending on conditions |
| Train | **UNVERIFIED** — check current Addis–Djibouti passenger service to Dire Dawa / connections |

### B8. Getting around

- **Inside Jugol:** Narrow winding alleys — **on foot only**.
- **Outside:** Bajaj and taxis for modern Harar and surrounds.
- **Do not expect** cars inside historic Jugol alleys.

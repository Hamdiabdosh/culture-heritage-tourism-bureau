## B. City facts

### B1. UNESCO

- **Name:** Harar Jugol, the Fortified Historic Town  
- **Inscribed:** 2006  
- **Criteria:** (ii), (iii), (iv), (v)  
- **Core zone:** 48 ha (entire walled city)  
- **Source:** [UNESCO WHC 1189](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1189/)

### B2. Why Harar matters

- **Faith:** Widely described as “fourth holy city” of Islam; UNESCO **82 mosques** (three from 10th c.), **102 shrines**  
- **Trade:** Late 16th–19th c. link coast ↔ Ethiopian interior; Islamic learning centre  
- **Living culture:** Functioning community — language, social systems, weaving/basketry, domestic architecture  

### B3. City of Peace / hospitality

- UNESCO Cities for Peace Prize cited as **2003** (also 2002–2003 in story pack) — verify  
- Frame as lived coexistence + community institutions, not generic marketing  
- Avoid “uniformly harmonious” language  

### B4. Scale visitors notice

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Walls | 13th–16th c. defensive walls; 16th-c. Islamic-town layout |
| Gates | **Five historic** + later **Harar Gate** |
| Mosques/shrines | 82 / 102 as heritage-inventory figures |
| Feel | Compact maze of alleys, facades, faith sites, markets, houses |

### B5. Seasons / climate

- Highland plateau; milder than Dire Dawa lowland  
- **Oct–Feb:** usually drier, comfortable walking  
- **Mar–Oct:** rain more likely; peak around **August**  
- Warm days / cool–mild mornings; check current weather  

### B6. Safety & respect

- Modest dress (shoulders/knees baseline)  
- Ask before photographing people, homes, worshippers, interiors  
- Shoes off where required; quiet during prayer; follow caretakers  
- Normal urban precautions; check official travel advisories  
- Local guide especially useful in labyrinth lanes  

### B7. Arrival

| Mode | Detail |
|---|---|
| Air + road | Dire Dawa gateway → Harar road |
| Dire Dawa–Harar | Commonly **45–60 min** (varies) |
| Addis by road | UNESCO ~**525 km**; most of a day; often via Dire Dawa |
| Train | Dire Dawa on rail network — schedules/ticketing **UNVERIFIED** |

### B8. Getting around

- Jugol on foot only (interior)  
- Bajaj outside walls  
- Do not expect cars through interior lanes  
- Harar Gate useful for central approach; historic gates for older road logic

- **Category:** Mosques & Shrines

## D. Experiences (8)

### D1. Three-Round Coffee Ceremony

- **Category:** Experience · Cultural & Spiritual  
- **Rounds:** *abol*, *tona*, *baraka* (bereka) — stay for all three  
- **Duration:** 1–3 hours  
- **Contact:** Visitor desk / local host  

### D2. Evening Hyena Feeding

- **Category:** Experience · Wildlife & Adventure  
- **Sites:** Near Fallana Gate; outside eastern wall near Sheikh Aw Anser shrine  
- **Timing:** From ~**19:00** (varies)  
- **Framing:** Cultural practice with wild animals — not a show; observation preferred  
- **Ashura link:** Some accounts connect ritual feeding to Ashura / Sufi traditions  

### D3. Harari Basket-Weaving Visit

- **Category:** Experience · Artisans & Crafts  
- **Duration:** 30–90 min; mornings best  
- **Contact:** Visitor desk / local host  

### D4. Market Walk and Spice Tasting

- **Category:** Experience · Food & Markets  
- **Duration:** 1–2 hours; ~08:00–11:00  
- **Notes:** Berbere blends; khat present in local commerce — don’t push visitors to buy/consume  

### D5. Night Walk Through Jugol

- **Category:** Experience · Cultural & Spiritual  
- **Duration:** 1–2 hours from sunset; guided  
- **Contact:** Visitor desk / local host  

### D6. Harari Cultural House Visit

- **Category:** Experience · Cultural & Spiritual  
- **Duration:** 45–90 min; pairs with coffee  
- **Contact:** Visitor desk / local host  

### D7. Harari Language and Daily-Life Exchange

- **Category:** Experience · Cultural & Spiritual  
- **Duration:** 30–60 min  
- **Note:** Informal; compensate fairly if paid  

### D8. Craft and Coffee Shopping Walk

- **Category:** Experience · Artisans & Crafts

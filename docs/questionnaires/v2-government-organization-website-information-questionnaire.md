# Government Organization Website Information Questionnaire — V2

## Purpose

This questionnaire is used to understand an organization's structure, responsibilities, services, leadership, content, branding, and website requirements before building or customizing its official website.

The questionnaire should preferably be completed **together with an authorized representative of the organization**.

The information collected will later be used to:

1. Understand the organization.
2. Build its organizational structure in the website.
3. Customize the website design and branding.
4. Collect and organize public content.
5. Populate the CMS.
6. Review and approve content before publication.

---

# IMPORTANT RULES

## Rule 1 — Do Not Guess

If information is not known, do not invent it.

Use:

**Not Provided**

or

**Needs Confirmation**

instead.

Never use assumptions such as:

* Standard government working hours
* Standard government email
* Assumed mission
* Assumed responsibilities
* Assumed legal basis
* Assumed official titles

unless the organization confirms them.

---

## Rule 2 — Separate Fact From Draft

Information supplied by the organization is considered source information.

AI may help:

* organize information
* clean grammar
* summarize
* translate
* structure content
* suggest website presentation

AI must not silently invent official facts.

---

## Rule 3 — Every Important Fact Has a Status

Use one of the following:

| Status                | Meaning                                       |
| --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| ✅ Confirmed           | Officially confirmed by the organization      |
| 🟡 Needs Verification | Information exists but needs confirmation     |
| 🔴 Not Provided       | Organization has not provided the information |
| ⚪ Not Applicable      | Does not apply                                |

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

The questionnaire is **not the website**.

It is the source from which the website is built.

The process should be:

**Discover → Verify → Structure → Customize → Enter → Review → Approve → Publish**

Never skip verification.

Never invent official information.

Never force an organization into a structure that does not represent its real organization.

The website should adapt to the organization—not the organization to the website.
